OUR LITTLE STORY
================

1. Extract (unzip) the ZIP file.
2. Open the "Our-Little-Story" folder.
3. Double-click "index.html".
4. Enjoy the proposal. Nothing to install and no internet connection needed.

Tips
- Keep the folder together: index.html needs the "assets" folder next to it.
- Best on a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on phone or computer.
- On a phone, open the folder in your Files app and tap index.html
  (some phones may ask you to choose a browser).

Optional music (you add it; none is included)
- Put an audio file you have the rights to in assets/audio/ (for example song.mp3).
- Open assets/js/script.js in any text editor and set:  var MUSIC_FILE = "assets/audio/song.mp3";
- Music only starts after the first tap on "Open your little surprise". A music button appears
  so it can be muted. With no file set, there is no music button.

Changing the words
- Open index.html in a text editor (Notepad, TextEdit) and edit the text between the tags.
